## ⚖️ Scoring Criteria & Evaluation Methodology

Each contestant was rigorously assessed across four fundamental performance criteria:
1. **Looks & Styling (10 Pts)**: Costume aesthetics, grooming, presence, and overall styling.
2. **Ramp Walk & Poise (10 Pts)**: Runway posture, pace, turns, balance, and rhythm.
3. **Stage Confidence (10 Pts)**: Eye contact, expression, stage command, and composure under spotlights.
4. **Personal Interaction (10 Pts)**: Introduction, clarity, spontaneity, and charm.

$$\text{Grand Total} = \text{Kaushal Manda (40)} + \text{Judge Ashritha (40)} + \text{Judge Eshan (40)} + \text{Judge Prasanna (40)} = \mathbf{160\text{ Max}}$$

$$\text{Result Status} = \begin{cases} \mathbf{QUALIFIED}, & \text{if Grand Total} \ge 100.0 \\ \mathbf{FAIL / ELIMINATED}, & \text{if Grand Total} < 100.0 \end{cases}$$
